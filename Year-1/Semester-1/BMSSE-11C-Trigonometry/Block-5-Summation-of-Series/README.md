## Learning Objectives
- Master techniques for summing trigonometric series
- Apply standard series (binomial, exponential, logarithmic, Gregory's)
- Use the difference method for series summation
- Evaluate complex trigonometric sums

## Key Concepts
- Binomial series: (1+x)ⁿ expansion
- Exponential series: eˣ = 1 + x + x²/2! + x³/3! + ...
- Logarithmic series: log(1+x) = x - x²/2 + x³/3 - ...
- Gregory's series: tan⁻¹x = x - x³/3 + x⁵/5 - ...
- Difference method: expressing general term as difference of consecutive terms
