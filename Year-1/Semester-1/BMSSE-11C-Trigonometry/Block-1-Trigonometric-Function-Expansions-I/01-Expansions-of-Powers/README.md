# Unit 1 — Expansions of cos^n θ and sin^n θ

## Topics
- Expansions of powers of sin θ
- Expansions of powers of cos θ
- Expansions of cos nθ and sin nθ

## Key Formulas

### De Moivre's Theorem
(cos θ + i sin θ)^n = cos nθ + i sin nθ

### Expansions of cos nθ
- cos 2θ = 2cos²θ - 1 = 1 - 2sin²θ
- cos 3θ = 4cos³θ - 3cos θ
- cos 4θ = 8cos⁴θ - 8cos²θ + 1
- cos nθ in terms of powers of cos θ

### Expansions of sin nθ
- sin 2θ = 2sin θ cos θ
- sin 3θ = 3sin θ - 4sin³θ
- sin 4θ = 4sin θ cos θ - 8sin³θ cos θ
- sin nθ in terms of powers of sin θ and cos θ

### Powers in Terms of Multiple Angles
- cos²θ = (1 + cos 2θ)/2
- sin²θ = (1 - cos 2θ)/2
- cos³θ = (3cos θ + cos 3θ)/4
- sin³θ = (3sin θ - sin 3θ)/4

## Important Techniques
1. Using De Moivre's theorem
2. Binomial expansion
3. Equating real and imaginary parts
4. Using Pascal's triangle coefficients
