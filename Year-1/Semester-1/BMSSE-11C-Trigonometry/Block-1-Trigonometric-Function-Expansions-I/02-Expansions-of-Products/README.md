# Unit 2 — Expansions of sin^m θ cos^n θ

## Topics

## Key Formulas

### Product-to-Sum
- sin A cos B = ½[sin(A+B) + sin(A-B)]
- cos A cos B = ½[cos(A+B) + cos(A-B)]
- sin A sin B = ½[cos(A-B) - cos(A+B)]

### Product Expansions
- cos mθ sin nθ expanded in terms of sines of multiple angles
- cos mθ cos nθ expanded in terms of cosines of multiple angles
- sin mθ sin nθ expanded in terms of cosines of multiple angles
