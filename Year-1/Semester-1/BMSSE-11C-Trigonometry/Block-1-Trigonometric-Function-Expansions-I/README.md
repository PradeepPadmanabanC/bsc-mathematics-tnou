# Block-I — Trigonometric Function Expansions-I

**Textbook Reference:** Chapter 2, Section 2.1, 2.1.1, 2.1.2.2.1.3

## Topics Covered
- Expansions of powers of sin θ, cos θ
- Expansions of cos nθ, sin nθ, cos mθ sin nθ

## Units
1. [01-Expansions-of-Powers](./01-Expansions-of-Powers/) — Expansions of cos^n θ and sin^n θ
2. [02-Expansions-of-Products](./02-Expansions-of-Products/) — Expansions of sin^m θ cos^n θ

## Learning Objectives
- Master expansions of powers of trigonometric functions
- Understand De Moivre's theorem applications
- Express cos nθ and sin nθ in terms of powers
- Work with product expansions

## Key Techniques
- De Moivre's theorem: (cos θ + i sin θ)^n = cos nθ + i sin nθ
- Binomial expansion and equating real/imaginary parts
- Product-to-sum formulas
