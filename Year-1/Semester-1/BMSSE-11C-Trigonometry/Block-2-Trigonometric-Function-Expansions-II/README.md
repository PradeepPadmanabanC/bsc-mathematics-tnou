# Block-II — Trigonometric Function Expansions-II

**Textbook Reference:** Chapter 3, Section 3.1 to 3.6

## Topics Covered
- Expansions of sin nθ, cos nθ, tan nθ
- Expansions of tan(θ₁ + θ₂ + ... + θₙ)
- Expansions of sin x, cos x, tan x in terms of x
- Sum of roots of trigonometric equations
- Formation of equation with trigonometric roots

- Master Maclaurin series for trigonometric functions

- Understand tangent addition formulas
- Solve problems involving roots of trigonometric equations
- Form equations from given trigonometric roots

## Key Concepts
- Taylor/Maclaurin series
- Multiple angle formulas for tan nθ
- Vieta's formulas for trigonometric equations
- Symmetric functions of roots
